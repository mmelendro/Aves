## Working rules

Use the existing shared map, gallery, carousel and account patterns. Keep region IDs, bird/place records, source-specific credits and honest unknown/empty states. Each remaining Australian collection needs five distinct real photographs with reviewed locality, rights and captions. Country overviews curate those same regional objects.

Read [the current handoff](docs/HANDOFF-CURRENT.md) before implementation. [Detailed product criteria](docs/ROADMAP-DETAILS.md), [the traveller workspace specification](docs/TRAVELLER-WORKSPACE-ROADMAP.md) and [creative direction](docs/AVES-CREATIVE-DIRECTION.md) are reference material, not competing queues. Older status and next-step notices do not override this list.

Use the existing Eco-Tourism Business Plan as the starting point for item 27. Apply documented stewardship evidence to directory and business-plan work. The [B Lab stewardship reference](https://usca.bcorporation.net/putting-stewardship-to-work-tracking-esc-standards/) is a reference to check when doing that work, not a claim that AVES or every listed supplier is certified.

Keep private traveller information and sensitive wildlife locations out of public content. Future models, game features, community sharing, paid integrations and outreach require their own concrete implementation scopes. No new purchase, invitation, automatic booking or public journal publication is created by this list.
