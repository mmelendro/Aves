# AVES-069 directory-led visitor-business sourcing

Owner clarification, 10 November 2026: use the existing B Corp directory for
travel, hospitality, accommodation, restaurants and related visitor businesses.
The earlier single retail candidate is a provenance test, not the discovery
strategy or a representative tourism shortlist. This direction supersedes that
candidate's prioritisation. No completed map layer or exhaustive list is claimed.

## Master discovery source

Global directory: https://www.bcorporation.net/en-us/find-a-b-corp/
Regional guidance: https://bcorporation.com.au/find-b-corps/
B Lab confirms keyword/location search:
https://www.bcorporation.net/en-us/faq/are-there-b-corps-my-area/

Use directory industry plus keyword and country filters. Keep AVES visitor-facing
categories separate from the directory's exact industry labels. “Ecotourism” is
not inferred merely from B Corp certification. Certification status, sector,
operating countries and location geometry are separate facts.

## Bulk-data finding that changes the approach

B Lab's 2024 primary article describes its then-public B Corp Impact Data dataset:
https://www.bcorporation.net/en-us/news/blog/research-fellowship-call-for-proposals-2024/

The old dataset URL https://data.world/blab/b-corp-impact-data now redirects to
https://data.world/community/goodbye/. The platform says its Open Data Community
retired on13 July2026 and community datasets are no longer available for download.
Do not propose that old download/API as an available current master feed.

A current supported B Lab export/feed/API and its commercial redistribution terms
have not yet been verified. Next sourcing work is to establish an approved current
bulk-access path (or a bounded manual directory review), then collect a coherent
visitor-sector/country batch. Prepare a B Lab data-access enquiry if useful, but
do not send without explicit owner instructions. Do not scrape around access
controls or create a paid provider/account to substitute for an unverified feed.

## Map architecture and stewardship

Maintain certified company records and separately sourced public location records.
Join them with explicit certification scope. Filter by actual location and visitor
category; show company operating-country coverage separately when useful. Keep
source URLs, last-checked dates, precision and honest incomplete coverage. Existing
bcorpPinEligible guard keeps unverified coordinates unmapped and the layer off.
