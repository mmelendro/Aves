# Landscape rollout: roadmap items52–61

## Colombia:52–58

Five independently sourced photographs have been added for each of Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta, Caribbean, Pacific Coast and Chocó, Llanos, Amazonia and Colombian Massif. All eleven Colombian regional galleries now contain five photographs. The shared country overview alternates regions and takes two photographs from each, using the same credited records as the regional views.

Every new image retains its photographer, original file page, reuse terms, review date and original/rendition hashes. Captions describe the photograph. Historical imagery does not establish current access, services, ecological condition or sightings. A place name never establishes permission to visit a protected or culturally sensitive site.

A similarly named Estonian Utria result was rejected and replaced with a source explicitly identifying Ensenada de Utría in Colombia. Source dates remain unnormalised when incomplete or ambiguous. No Indigenous name, translation, pronunciation or audio has been invented.

Validation:684 tests passed, strict TypeScript passed, architecture audit passed with395 reachable source modules and no unreachable modules, and the 78-route production build passed. Physical Safari and independent visitor checks remain open.

## Australia:59–61

The candidate inventory records 45 distinct source photographs, five per Australian planning region, with photographer, source page, original URL, declared reuse terms and review date. It marks unselected candidates for further visual, locality and cultural-context review before publication. Results from New Zealand and images from outside Jervis Bay Territory were rejected for those inventory slots.

New South Wales and Queensland now each have five reviewed landscape photographs. They reuse the existing country/region gallery and map context. The Australia overview balances the two completed regional collections. The other Australian galleries remain pending.

A historical Daintree image has conflicting source/scan dates. Its capture date remains unnormalised rather than assigning the scan date to the landscape. Existing public-domain source declarations are linked explicitly; no new rights claim is made.
