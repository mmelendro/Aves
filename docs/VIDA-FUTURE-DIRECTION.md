# VIDA future direction

Recorded 11 November 2026 from Martin’s instruction. Long-term direction, not a current rebrand.

VIDA is the prospective umbrella for nature experiences. AVES remains the current birding brand. Ballenas is a possible whale-focused branch, with other nature experiences to follow.

One geographic explorer per page should connect relevant species, habitats, places, stays and locally led experiences. Geography should not be duplicated for each future nature category.

Commercial development should pair a clear, personally confirmed trip-planning offer with conservation benefit and local livelihoods. Do not claim B Corp certification, verified regeneration, partner status or quantified impact without evidence.

Future discovery work: validate demand, pricing and margins; agree local partner and guide terms; define measurable conservation and community outcomes; review the applicable B Corp standards before any claim or certification plan. No new paid services or spending beyond existing safeguards are authorized by this roadmap.
