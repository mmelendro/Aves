# Reviewed names and landscape galleries

The next sequence covers Colombia galleries, Australia galleries, gallery consistency, route references, conservation and B Corp directories, bird filters, seasonal planning, private albums, image rights, launch checks and the updated business plan. Each increment is reviewed and tested before release.

Names and pronunciation remain part of this work. Keep original spelling and diacritics, cite language and community sources, distinguish historical names from current naming, and link permitted pronunciation recordings when available. Do not invent names, language attribution or pronunciation.

This increment adds five distinct Central Andes landscapes with source, licence and photographer credit. Colombia now has a country preview and a regional gallery. Regions awaiting photographs retain a route back to the available gallery and map.

Two name examples begin the shared place and bird display: Kumanday, historically attributed to the Quimbaya by Colombia’s national parks authority, and kea, with a pronunciation reference at Te Aka Māori Dictionary. No audio is copied or generated.
