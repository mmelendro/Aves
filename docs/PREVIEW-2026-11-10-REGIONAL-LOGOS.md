# Regional bird logo increment

10 November 2026. Owner requested matching regional/subregional identities, all
birds facing right, and new artwork as needed. All changes remain candidate source
and preview work. No production publish is included.

Coverage: three country identities, all23 existing planning regions and all four
South Island place contexts. The four country/global originals remain unchanged;
a single artwork-direction rule mirrors them at render time.25 new transparent
right-facing illustrations were generated with the built-in image tool, reviewed
for direction/major species features and packaged as320px WebP assets (481,160 bytes
total). Original generated PNGs are retained outside the served repository assets.
Prompts, source filenames, web hashes and sizes are recorded in
content/regional-brand-art-provenance.json. Credits disclose generated illustration.
These are brand illustrations, not documentary field-identification plates.

Each region's representative is an editorial choice for distinctive appearance
and local association, not a scientific ranking of recognisability. Endemic or
restricted-range birds do not imply occurrence throughout a planning region.
Source/rationale records are in content/regional-brand-birds.json. No booking,
conservation-status, availability or sighting claim is added.

One BrandBird renderer owns direction for headers, footers, resource cards and
travel-player marks. BrandMark resolves the same existing public selection in each
placement, with direct-route fallback and no persistence. Unknown places fall back
to their region, then their own country; countries cannot leak into each other.
Resource/player artwork follows its programme/place rather than the page behind
it. Canterbury soundscapes retain broad South Island identity and do not borrow
Fiordland/Otago locality from the currently browsed map. Decorative footer contours
reuse the existing reviewed terrain and omit water for inland contexts.

Navigation, home/subtitle sibling links, media playback and separate application
boundaries remain. No providers, account writes, billing changes or dependencies.

Automated validation:183 main checks,310 Australia checks, strict TypeScript and
both full production builds passed before the final credits disclosure. Hosted
candidate/browser acceptance is recorded below once completed. Physical hardware
is not implied. Sitemap route registry is unchanged; credits is an existing route.
