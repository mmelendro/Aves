## Current owner direction

One continuous outline connects the New Zealand landscape beneath the logo to
Japanese-inspired wave crests on the right under the menu. This outline itself
is the header/body boundary, with the actual page background emerging beneath
it. No separate divider, parallel water strokes or duplicate logo contour.
Mountain shoulders are rounded to share the waves' flowing style. The entire
outline uses one emerald, 1px, rounded-join/cap stroke at a single opacity.
Mobile reuses the same shape with lower wave amplitude and responsive geometry.
Sparse birds were offered as optional, but are not added to this revision.

Earlier separate-line, angular-edge and near-flat-horizon previews are
superseded. Original bird PNGs, wordmark, country links and footer remain intact.
Only the header's independent contour is hidden while the connected outline is
shown. Main global/NZ and Colombian header templates opt in, with the rounded
NZ silhouette specifically refined. Australia/private portal and unified draft
are unchanged. Body begins 28px behind the boundary. No new assets/dependencies,
account/data/provider changes or animation. ResizeObserver disconnects on cleanup.
