# AVES-061 New Zealand alpine/coastal header

10 November 2026, Sydney. Owner approved the final concept and explicitly requested
publication. This document supersedes the early rounded-wave previews.

The implementation uses original SVG paths rather than the generated concept image.
Two unequal Southern Alps-inspired summits share one skyline. Short ridge/gully
marks suggest snow/rock structure without scalloped snowcaps. The kea faces right.
The wordmark is measured so its centre follows the midpoint of the two summits.
One main boundary continues into one low swell with short interior water ridges.
A small exposed whale back/dorsal and fluke use finer lines. Nine small tern-inspired
birds form a loose, static feeding group; four remain in the compact header.
No left flock, motion, artificial vortex or literal geographic panorama is claimed.

ResizeObserver measures the wordmark and navigation, including font completion.
Artwork uses CSS-pixel geometry rather than stretching animals with the screen.
The small-screen Radix menu preserves native destination, planning and account links,
keyboard focus handling, Escape and 44px targets. Decorative SVG ignores input.
No dependencies, backend, map artwork, media behaviour or privacy settings change.

Release scope is the public NZ country and South Island headers only. The global,
Colombian and Australian templates retain their published appearance. Footer branding
is preserved. Other geographies require their own evidence and water/wildlife choices.

Source baseline: main 30f6925 / runtime 8c7f297. Protected preview and final release
verification are recorded separately when complete. Existing 149 tests, two new
responsive geometry checks, TypeScript and local production build passed. Local
browser preview launcher rejected Next.js-incompatible flags; use protected hosted
preview for browser review. Physical iPhone/Safari remains unverified.

The owner also requested a reusable natural-context design process and regional
rollout after this launch. That is a separate reviewed source increment, not automatic
permission to publish unreviewed country artwork or consolidate the two applications.
