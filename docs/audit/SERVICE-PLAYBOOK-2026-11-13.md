# AVES service playbook

Owner working draft for roadmap81. This is a proposed workflow, not a public response-time promise. Actual support hours, available capacity and backup contacts remain unconfirmed. Read alongside the November Eco-Tourism Business Plan. No paid service launch or appointment is inferred.

## Before accepting a paid order

Record the contracting business, scoped corridor and deliverable, agreed fee and payment terms, founder hours available after other commitments, nonbillable hours, maximum active orders, support window with time zone, response target within that window, backup person and consent, cancellation terms and emergency boundaries. Unknown fields stay unknown. Do not accept an order exceeding funded, confirmed delivery capacity.

Capacity ceiling = floor((available monthly hours − nonbillable hours − support reserve hours) / measured delivery hours per order). It is a ceiling, not a safe sales target. Set a smaller operating cap after the pilot. Count founder pay from month one and separate customer/supplier obligations from unrestricted cash.

## Enquiry to handover

| Trigger | Action | Completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New enquiry | Capture route, dates, group, interests, access needs, budget currency and decision date | Saved private brief with missing decisions visible |
| Scope agreed | State deliverable, revision allowance, turnaround, contact window and exclusions | Owner-approved scope and traveller agreement |
| Supplier reference | Distinguish public listing from written participation and confirmed booking | Dated source, agreement or supplier confirmation, correctly labelled |
| Preparation | Check current source dates, route pacing, seasonal access, transport and costs | Source-linked itinerary with gaps and alternatives |
| Review | Keep traveller edits, record decisions and price any added scope | Dated accepted revision |
| Handover | Export selected private trip pack and identify who confirms each open arrangement | Dated snapshot and unresolved-action list |
| Completion | Record all hours, direct cost, realised fee, revisions, feedback and refund exposure | Updated unit economics and next capacity decision |

## Disruption handling

| Situation | Immediate handling | Owner of next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Booking unconfirmed | Label requested/provisional. Do not imply availability or acceptance | Booking party obtains direct supplier confirmation |
| Access or weather disruption | Check official source and supplier, pause affected activity and present alternatives | Traveller and named delivery supplier agree changes |
| Medical or immediate danger | Direct traveller to local emergency services or their insurer using destination-verified contacts in their actual trip pack | Local emergency responders, insurer and traveller |
| Missed transfer or supplier failure | Record time, source and booking party. Contact the agreed provider inside the actual service scope | Confirmed provider, with AVES role stated in agreement |
| Complaint/refund | Acknowledge within the agreed support window, preserve evidence and identify applicable agreement | Contracting party reviews and communicates decision |
| Founder unavailable | Pause new commitments and use only the previously agreed backup | Named backup, once confirmed and authorised |
| Privacy correction | Restrict access, record the issue and follow the established incident process | Account/data owner and designated operator |

Never advertise emergency monitoring or continuous support without funded coverage. No message is sent by this document.

## Stewardship evidence

Following B Lab ESC guidance, record corridor biodiversity/access risks, a dated improvement target, reuse/waste decisions, wildlife care, and supplier review evidence. Track actual local supplier spend and contributor consent. Do not imply certification, cultural authority or measured positive impact from a listing. Review after each pilot and monthly before expansion.

Source: https://usca.bcorporation.net/putting-stewardship-to-work-tracking-esc-standards/ (reviewed13November2026Sydney). Adaptation for a small planning service, not a certification assessment.
