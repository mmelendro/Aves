# Private pilot operating rules

Adopted for the current private development stage on 14 November 2026, following the instruction to finish the remaining work. These rules do not approve a paid launch, a public journal, any individual media item, an invitation or a new charge.

## Audience, media and review

1. Trip records, journals, originals and contributor information remain owner-only. Existing public reference media retains its existing licence and attribution.
2. Before selecting a new item for public display, record the exact file, actual creator, permission or licence, required credit, modification and intended audience. Folder access and a file owner are not evidence of copyright permission.
3. A filename is a review lead. Confirm the bird, capture date and broad place separately. Group checklists and totals never become the owner's individual sightings. Unknown fields remain unknown.
4. Withhold nesting/roosting locations, precise sensitive wildlife coordinates, participant contacts, financial records and booking details from previews intended for others. A future public item must receive a separate factual, rights and sensitivity review before publication.
5. The owner selects the exact preview and audience. An invitation or new share requires an explicit person-directed instruction. Review access, correction and removal requests through the existing enquiry route. No automatic public moderation or verification award is introduced.

## Measurement and spending

Use a manual review register for volunteered pilot sessions. Record only the minimum agreed task results and aggregate observations; no new analytics, advertising, identifiers or background tracking is installed.

| Measure | Definition | Initial baseline | Review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Planning completion | Agreed planning steps completed / attempted, with blockers | Unknown | After each trial |
| Saving and return | Whether the same volunteer can save, reopen and export the selected plan | Unknown | During the trial |
| Delivery effort | Actual preparation, revision, support and administration minutes per deliverable | Unknown | Each completed example |
| Cash contribution | Earned, collected fee less actual variable cash cost; separately show founder pay and fixed costs | Unknown | Each order, monthly aggregate |
| Supplier evidence | Planned supplier references with dated, scoped environmental evidence / references reviewed | Unknown | Before handover |
| Evidence quality | Unresolved access, rights, wildlife-location and environmental-claim exceptions | Unknown | Before handover |

The project owner maintains the register and reviews it monthly. Do not infer environmental outcomes from supplier coverage, or estimate carbon offsets without a defined boundary and supporting data. Keep device reuse and avoidable download volume in the stewardship review. These proportionate measures follow the [B Lab ESC reference](https://usca.bcorporation.net/putting-stewardship-to-work-tracking-esc-standards/); they are not a certification claim.

The incremental recurring-spend limit for this work remains zero. An existing subscription is not authority for a new plan or branch charge. Quote a proposed cost and obtain a specific spending decision before enabling it. Available delivery hours, prices, support windows and commercial capacity remain unconfirmed owner facts.
