# Map-layer rollout criteria

AVES-068 safe continuity is published and paused. This file retains source and
projection criteria, not an automatic rollout instruction. Read HANDOFF-CURRENT.md
and NEXT-WEEK-PROMPT.md for delivered scope and permission to proceed.

## South Island sample

Satellite, ecosystems, national parks and DOC conservation land combine in the
same Mercator frame. The combined pilot adds five independent public-place
categories: nature/birding places, conservation projects, public heritage,
cities/towns and ecotourism businesses. Public places are a curated starting
selection, not a full directory. Species habitat selection remains separate.
The matching release note records publication and browser verification.

## Next appropriate rollouts

1. Expand through NZ country/island views using the full DOC public-land source,
   correctly projected satellite frames and Natural Earth settlement references.
   Obtain North Island/Rakiura ecological coverage and local public-place
   evidence before exposing those facets there. Never label South Island-only
   records as whole-NZ coverage.
2. Australia: use CAPAD 2024 recognised terrestrial protected areas, excluding
   interim records. Check the actual legal national-park category, not IUCN II
   as a substitute. Preserve the existing state/territory geometry and links.
   Its current Albers overview cannot receive a stretched Mercator raster;
   use correctly transformed geometry or an explicit optional geographic view.
3. Colombia: retain the original internal planning-region artwork and its
   checked marker positions. The artwork is not georeferenced official land
   boundaries. Use RUNAP's official public geometry and its CC BY-SA 4.0 terms
   for protected areas, with its own projection/alignment and attribution.
   Never drape precise satellite or RUNAP coordinates over hand-drawn planning
   artwork by guessing a bounding box. Keep planning and geographic contexts
   clearly distinguished, with all existing region URLs intact.
4. Add country/subregion public nature, heritage and ecotourism entries from
   primary local sources. Record coordinate source/precision, check date,
   conservation evidence and whether claims are operator-reported. No ranking,
   B Corp certification, AVES partnership, guaranteed sightings or booking is
   implied by inclusion. Distinguish tourism enterprises from projects, lodges,
   protected areas and global ecological biodiversity hotspots.
5. Audit existing native/introduced and other species facets before expanding.
   Status must be local to geography/taxonomy. Month/season filters require
   sourced observation coverage and effort; do not infer introduced from
   migration or fabricate month data. Preserve private saved wishes.

## Available research and limits

Public source candidates are already identified: DOC Public Conservation Land
FeatureServer; DCCEEW CAPAD 2024; PNN's official RUNAP download; NASA GIBS Blue
Marble historical 2004; Natural Earth 5.1.2 populated places; Zenodo biodiversity
hotspots 2016.1. A source URL alone is not implemented coverage. Verify fields,
licence, date, projection, geometry and visitor-access limitations before use.
Keep attribution and derived-data licence with each output. Do not include
private owners, sensitive wildlife points or restricted cultural sites.

Soundscapes AVES-053 remains the next media priority, with licensed provenance,
visitor opt-in, volume/mute and coordinated fading. Google routes/travel times
AVES-062 is retained separately; no paid mapping key/service is authorised.
No database work or app-root merge is required for these map layers.

## Business-plan and stewardship checks

The existing South Island planning brief requires evidence for local benefit,
supplier claims, fair commercial terms and operational responsibility before
marketplace/booking expansion. This public reference layer establishes none of
those commercial arrangements. Track evidence and unknowns rather than awarding
an unsourced sustainable label. This supports the evidence and supplier review
direction in B Lab's ESC guidance:
https://usca.bcorporation.net/putting-stewardship-to-work-tracking-esc-standards/

## Release gate for each increment

Type/build checks, actual layered selection flow, desktop/narrow/keyboard
verification, source links, human/XML sitemaps and private-route exclusions.
Save the exact previous and new runtime, named recovery branch and deployment.
No free-credit AI retry, new spend, protection bypass or broad secret pull.
